## 1. Your contribution and ownership

A contribution is code, documentation or other work you author and submit
in a pull request to the isoboard repository at
[github.com/carlesonielfa/isoboard-app](https://github.com/carlesonielfa/isoboard-app),
from the time you accept this agreement until you withdraw it under section 5.
It does not cover issues, discussions, independent forks, or material you mark
in the pull request as excluded or "not a contribution".

You retain ownership and may use or license your own work elsewhere. This is a
non-exclusive license, not a transfer of ownership. Neither party must accept,
publish, maintain, support or pay for a contribution under this agreement.

## 4. Authority and other material

You confirm that you have authority to grant these permissions for each
contribution. If rights belong to an employer or another organization, obtain its
authorization and tell the maintainer at the licensing email before the
contribution is merged; an individual acceptance does not by itself bind that
organization. An authorized representative may accept on its behalf by email,
identifying the organization and capacity. Work by another person is covered
only if that person also accepts this agreement or otherwise grants permission.

In the pull request, identify third-party material, its origin, license and any
restrictions before inclusion. Your grant covers only rights you control; third-party material keeps
its own terms. Disclose any known restriction or rights issue affecting the grant,
and notify the maintainer if you later discover that your authority statement
was materially inaccurate.

Apart from these statements, the contribution is provided as is, without promises
of performance, fitness or absence of defects or third-party claims, to the extent
permitted by law. You have no duty to provide support or indemnify the maintainer
or recipients. This agreement adds no contractual liability cap and excludes no

## 5. Acceptance, withdrawal and later changes

You accept by posting, from your GitHub account on an isoboard pull request, the
acceptance comment that the repository's agreement check requests, which names
this version. You accept once; later pull requests need no new acceptance. The
maintainer records your GitHub account, the pull request, the comment and its
time. A Git sign-off or checkbox alone is not acceptance. Send private identity or
organization-authorization details to the licensing email, not a public comment.
Agreement records are handled as described in the
[privacy notice](src-tauri/legal/PRIVACY-NOTICE.md).

You may withdraw this agreement for future pull requests by notice to the
licensing email. Withdrawal does not affect contributions submitted before it.
A later version of this agreement applies only if you accept it; until then,
this version continues to cover your contributions. Changes to price, support or
release versions must remain within the grant in this agreement; a change
exceeding it requires separate permission. Neither a changed branch nor a later
edit of a public comment changes the agreement you accepted.
