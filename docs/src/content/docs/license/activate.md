You buy an isoboard commercial license through Polar checkout, which emails you a license key. Paste the key into the **Settings** tab → **License** in isoboard. After a successful online check, the license works offline and the app shows "Licensed for personal and commercial use."

## How do I buy a license?

1. Open the **Settings** tab and find the **License** section. Select **Buy a license**. isoboard opens Polar checkout in your default browser.
2. Review the price, currency, taxes and total shown at checkout. The price is [€50 per user](../pricing-and-licensing/).
3. Complete the payment. Polar is the seller and merchant of record.
4. Polar emails an order confirmation with a link to its customer portal. Your license key is in the portal.

You do not need an isoboard account. Opening checkout does not grant a license by itself. Keep your receipt and purchase email.

You can buy several seats in one checkout. Each seat is one named user. Polar's customer portal lets the buyer assign seats by entering each person's email address. Polar sends that person an invitation with a link to claim the seat, and the license key comes with it. The buyer can resend an invitation or revoke a seat in the portal to reassign it. See [Polar's seat-based pricing guide](https://polar.sh/docs/features/seat-based-pricing).

## How do I activate my license key?

1. Open the **Settings** tab and find the **License** section.
2. Paste your key into the **License key** field. Keys start with `ISOAPP-`.
3. Select the arrow button (**Activate commercial license**).

During first setup, the same key field appears on the license screen next to **Continue without a license key**.

Activation needs an internet connection. isoboard checks the key with Polar and stores it securely.
<!-- os:macos: Windows will use Credential Manager -->
On a Mac the key is stored in the macOS Keychain.

The check sends your key, product identifiers and app version to Polar. It sends no captures, device fingerprints or computer names. See [what leaves your Mac](../../privacy/data/).

## What does "Licensed for personal and commercial use" mean?

The **License** section shows this line once a license is active. It means isoboard verified your key for the covered major version, and you can use the app for work as well as personally. isoboard also shows when your Priority Email Support is active until, and an **Email support** button that opens a draft to support@isoboard.app. It does not send anything.

Before activation, the section shows one of these:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Commercial trial ends (date). Personal use is free. | The seven-day evaluation is running. |
| Free for personal use. License required for commercial use. | The evaluation ended or you have not started one. |

See [pricing and licensing](../pricing-and-licensing/) for what counts as personal and commercial use.

## Does isoboard work offline with a license?

Yes. After a successful first activation, isoboard keeps your commercial rights cached. It checks Polar in the background now and then. The license has no offline expiry.

A network failure or a Polar service error does not remove your license. Only a status change that Polar confirms, such as a refund, ends it. isoboard applies that at the next successful check. If a check fails, select **Retry** in the **License** section.

## How do I remove a license key?

1. Open the **Settings** tab and find the **License** section.
2. Select **Remove license**.

This removes the license from this Mac and deletes the stored key.
<!-- os:macos: Windows uses Credential Manager -->
On a Mac, the key is deleted from the macOS Keychain.

Removing it does not cancel your purchase, contact Polar, refund anything or restart the seven-day evaluation. Your captures and History stay.

## How do I move my license to another Mac?

The license is tied to a named user and has no hard device limit. It is not tied to one computer. Install isoboard on the other Mac, open the **Settings** tab → **License** and enter the same key. There is no device activation to release first. Use it only for the named user, on computers they are authorized to use.

Use **Remove license** on the old Mac if you want it off that Mac. This is optional.

## What if I lost my license key?

Polar receipts and order recovery are the first route. Open the customer portal link in your Polar order confirmation email to see your key again. If you cannot find the email, write to support@isoboard.app with your order reference and purchase email. Do not send payment-card details. Billing and license recovery help stays available after priority support ends.

If a key is rotated or no longer valid, replace it through the same key field.

## How do refunds work?

isoboard offers a voluntary full refund within 14 days of purchase, in addition to your statutory rights. To ask, email support@isoboard.app with your order reference and purchase email. A refund ends the refunded commercial license. Personal use stays available and your data is not deleted. EU and EEA consumers may also have a statutory withdrawal right.

The [purchase and refund terms](https://isoboard.app/legal/purchase-refunds/) set the process, deadlines and the withdrawal form, and they govern over this summary.

## What if activation fails?

- Check that you pasted the complete key, starting with `ISOAPP-`.
- Check your internet connection and select **Retry**.
- A failed replacement keeps your existing license.
- If the key is valid and the error stays, email support@isoboard.app with your app version and order reference.
