isoboard is source-available, not open source. You can read, modify and test the code, and personal use is free. Work use needs a €50 commercial license after a seven-day evaluation. These answers summarize the license. The linked legal terms govern if anything here differs.

**Is isoboard open source?**

No. isoboard is source-available. You can inspect, modify and test the code and share copies for free, but work use needs a paid license and selling or hosting it needs a separate agreement. Read the [source license](https://isoboard.app/legal/source-license/). See [pricing and licensing](../pricing-and-licensing/) for the price.

**Does my company need a license for every employee?**

Each person who uses isoboard for work needs a seat. A seat is a named user. People who only develop or test modifications do not need one. A seat can be reassigned when someone leaves, but not rotated between active users. You can buy several seats in one checkout and assign them by email. See [buy and activate a license](../activate/). A contractor can use their own license or a seat assigned by a client. See the [commercial terms](https://isoboard.app/legal/commercial-terms/) and [pricing and licensing](../pricing-and-licensing/).

**Do nonprofits and public institutions need a license?**

Yes. The source license counts nonprofit and public-sector work as commercial use, and an organization's profit status does not decide the category. See [pricing and licensing](../pricing-and-licensing/).

**Is isoboard free for students and teachers?**

Personal study and unpaid student coursework count as personal use, which is free. Paid teaching counts as commercial use and needs a license. There is no education or nonprofit discount currently.

**Can I modify isoboard or run my own fork?**

Yes. You can modify the code and keep private changes private. You can share copies and modified builds for free, but recipients stay bound by the license, including the paid-license requirement for work use. Mark modified builds as unofficial and describe the changes. Read the [branding and fork guidelines](https://isoboard.app/legal/branding-forks/).

**Does a license cover my fork or a hired developer's changes?**

Yes for isoboard code. A commercial license covers the isoboard code of the purchased major version, including in forks and changes by a hired developer. Code that someone adds under a separate license keeps its own terms. See the [commercial terms](https://isoboard.app/legal/commercial-terms/).

**Can I sell isoboard or offer it as a service?**

Not without a separate written agreement. Selling copies, bundling isoboard into a paid product or offering its features as a hosted service needs one, even if the service is free. Paid development, installation and support for properly licensed copies are allowed. Write to legal@isoboard.app.

**What happens to my license after a year?**

Your license for the covered major version does not expire. Priority email support lasts 12 months, and billing and recovery help continues afterwards. A future major version may need a separate purchase. See [buy and activate a license](../activate/).

**Can I get a refund?**

Yes. There is a voluntary 14-day full refund, additional to statutory rights. See [buy and activate a license](../activate/) and the [purchase and refund terms](https://isoboard.app/legal/purchase-refunds/).
