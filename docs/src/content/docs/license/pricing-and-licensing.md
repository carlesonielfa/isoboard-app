isoboard is free for personal use. Using it for work needs a commercial license, which costs €50 once per user and includes updates for the covered major version and 12 months of priority email support. Every new installation can evaluate work use for seven days before buying.

:::note
This page summarizes the license. The [commercial terms](https://isoboard.app/legal/commercial-terms/) and the [source license](https://isoboard.app/legal/source-license/) govern if anything here differs.
:::

## How much does isoboard cost?

| | Personal use | Work use |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | €50 per commercial license |
| Billing | None | One payment, no subscription |
| Evaluation | Not needed | Seven days from first use, then a license is required |
| Support | Community, through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/carlesonielfa/isoboard-app/issues) | 12 months of priority email support |

Polar is the seller and applies tax display for your region. In some regions the €50 already includes tax, and in others tax is added at checkout. Checkout shows the price, currency, taxes and total before you pay.

The license price does not include your AI provider. OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and other providers bill you directly. The local isOCR™ model has no usage fees. See [choose a local model or an AI provider](../../models/choose-a-model/).

## Is isoboard free to use?

Yes, for these uses:

- **Personal use.** Personal, non-commercial activities, including personal study and unpaid student coursework.
- **Development and testing.** You may inspect, copy, modify and test the code, including as paid or employer-sponsored development. This does not cover ordinary productive use of the app for work.
- **The seven-day evaluation** of work use, described below.

Personal use stays free after the evaluation ends. Capture, History and settings keep working.

## Can I use isoboard at work?

Yes, for seven days. After that, work use needs a commercial license. The terms define commercial use as use in employment, a trade or profession, or the work of an organization. That includes nonprofit and public-sector work, paid teaching and research employment. Who owns the computer and whether the organization makes a profit do not decide the category. If you mix personal and work use, the work use needs the license.

### How does the seven-day evaluation work?

1. On first setup, isoboard shows **Continue without a license key** and **Activate commercial license**.
2. Choose **Continue without a license key**. The evaluation starts then and lasts seven consecutive 24-hour periods.
3. Check **Settings** tab → **License** for the end date. It shows the dates in your local time.

The evaluation needs no account, payment card or network request. Updates, reinstalling and restarting setup do not start another evaluation.

After it ends, isoboard keeps working technically. The terms still require a commercial license for productive work use.

## What does a commercial license cover?

| Covered | Details |
|---|---|
| Seat | One named user. Each person who uses isoboard for work needs a seat. |
| Computers | Personal, employer-provided or client-provided computers the user is authorized to use, with no hard device limit. |
| Version | The major version named in your order, with all updates released within it. Future major versions may need a separate purchase. |
| Duration | Perpetual for that major version, subject to the terms. |
| Modified builds | The license covers isoboard code, including in forks and in changes made by a hired developer. Private modifications need not be published. |
| Support | 12 months of priority email support from the day the license is issued. |

The current version is 1.x.

Companies need one seat per commercial user. People who only develop or test modifications do not need a seat. Seats can move to another person when someone leaves or changes roles, but they must not rotate between active users to avoid licensing them. A contractor can use their own license or a seat assigned by a client.

The license does not allow selling copies of isoboard, bundling it into a paid product or offering its features as a hosted service. Those need a separate written agreement. Separately licensed model weights keep their own licenses.

## How long does priority email support last?

Priority email support lasts 12 calendar months from license issuance. It covers official releases, installation, configuration, licensing and reproducible defects. Priority means prioritized handling. It does not promise a response deadline, a guaranteed fix or custom development. Support goes to support@isoboard.app. Read the [support policy](https://isoboard.app/legal/support/) for details.

## What happens when support ends?

Your license keeps working. Support expiry does not end the license for the covered major version. Help with billing and license recovery stays available at the same address. No separate renewal of priority support is offered at launch.

## What happens if I refund or lose the license?

A full refund ends the refunded commercial license. Personal use stays available and your data is not deleted. isoboard learns about a confirmed refund at its next successful license check. See [buy and activate a license](../activate/) for refunds and recovery.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is isoboard free?**

Personal use is free. Using isoboard for work costs €50 per user after a seven-day evaluation. Development and testing of modifications are free.

**How much does isoboard cost?**

A commercial license is €50 once per user, with no subscription. Polar applies regional tax display, so tax is included or added at checkout depending on where you are. Your AI provider bills you separately.

**Can I use isoboard at work for free?**

Only during the seven-day evaluation. After that, a commercial license is required for work use, including at nonprofits and public institutions.
