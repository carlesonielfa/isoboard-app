## Modified builds

Clearly identify modified builds as unofficial and describe their changes. You may keep the isoboard name and icon if the unofficial status is clear in the app and wherever you offer the build. A distinct name and icon are optional. Do not imply that Carles Onielfa Pérez publishes, endorses or supports your build.

For example: **isoboard — unofficial build by Example Team**, accompanied by a description of the changes. You may also choose a different name and describe it as “Based on isoboard.” Preserve copyright, license and third-party notices in either case.

Private personal and internal company builds may also retain the name and icon, provided their users know they are modified and unofficial. No individual branding approval is required for these uses.

## Reviews, tutorials and factual references

You may use official screenshots and unmodified logos in reviews, tutorials and factual compatibility references without individual permission, provided they do not imply endorsement. These permissions cover only rights the licensor owns or controls, not other people's content or personal information in screenshots. No trademark ownership or broader brand rights are granted.

## Fork licensing and support

Free distribution remains subject to the source license. Preserve the isoboard code's terms and identify any separate terms for independently authored additions. An isoboard commercial seat covers isoboard code of the purchased major version, including in forks; separately licensed additions retain their own terms.

Selling distributions, bundling the app into a paid product or offering its functionality as a hosted service requires a separate written agreement. Genuine paid development, installation and support remain permitted within the source license's scope; they do not purchase an end user's commercial seat.

Your fork's support is separate from isoboard priority support. Do not advertise the licensor as providing custom-fork support. Official support may require reproducing an issue on an official release, as explained in the [support policy](SUPPORT.md).

For other brand uses, contact legal@isoboard.app. These guidelines do not narrow the source license, mandatory rights or independent third-party permissions.
