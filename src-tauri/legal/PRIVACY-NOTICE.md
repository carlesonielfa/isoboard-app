## Captures, processing and local storage

isoboard processes your selected images and instructions. With local models for
both conversion and Additional Rules, processing happens on your device. Downloads
and other network connections are described below.

Your local History may contain extracted content, model results, thumbnails,
cropped images and information about processing and errors. It does not retain
the original full screenshot as a History record. History is limited by the app's
record and storage limits, rather than a time-based expiry. You can delete entries
or clear History. Clearing History does not remove imported source files or copies
kept by your operating system, backups, clipboard managers or destination apps.

Settings, profiles, credentials and downloaded models are also stored on your
device. Uninstalling the app may leave local data behind. Installing isoboard does
not give us access to these files.

## External AI providers

If you choose an external AI provider, the relevant image, extracted text and
instructions are sent directly to that provider or your configured endpoint.
Additional Rules can send intermediate text to a separate provider. Using a remote
provider for either step may disclose captured content.

Connections use the API keys or other credentials you configure. Providers apply
their own terms and privacy practices, which may vary by account. Review them before
sending sensitive material; we do not promise that a provider never retains inputs
or uses them for training. Normal inference does not pass through an
isoboard-operated server.

## Downloads and external pages

Model downloads connect to Hugging Face and its delivery providers; model-catalog
updates connect to models.dev. They receive ordinary connection information such
as your IP address and the requested resource, but catalog updates do not include
your captures. Update checks and update downloads connect to GitHub, which receives
the same kind of connection information; they do not include your captures, and
you can turn off automatic checks in Settings. External pages, including Hugging
Face and GitHub, apply their own privacy practices.

## Website, storage and analytics

The website and its media are served using Cloudflare. Requests disclose
connection information such as IP address and requested resource to the services
handling them.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for website audience and performance measurement
with Cloudflare's regional exclusion enabled. The browser analytics beacon is
excluded for connections served through Cloudflare data centres in the EU, the
wider EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. This is based on connection
routing, not the visitor's nationality or residence. Hosting and security
processing still occurs for visitors in those regions.

See the [website storage and analytics notice](WEBSITE-STORAGE.md) for the
verified technologies, purposes, durations and any available controls.

## Purchases, activation and support

Polar handles checkout and stores purchase and license records. We use customer
contact details, order information, seat assignments, license status and payment
or refund information for licensing, support and required records. Payment-card
details are handled by Polar and its payment providers.

The app sends your license key, product identifiers, app version and connection
information, including your IP address, to Polar for validation. These checks do
not send captures, device fingerprints, computer names or usage counts. The app
stores credentials and license status locally for activation and offline use;
removing a local license does not cancel the purchase or delete Polar's records.
We do not maintain a separate customer database or server-side activation log.

Apple iCloud Mail stores correspondence sent to legal@isoboard.app and
support@isoboard.app, including contact details, messages and attachments. We do
not automatically forward it or keep separate email archives or backups outside
iCloud. The app does not automatically send captures to support. Avoid sending
credentials or unnecessary sensitive information, and review diagnostics before
sharing them. GitHub issues are public; send private purchase information by email.

When you accept the Contributor Agreement on a pull request, an automated check
records your GitHub username and account ID, the pull request number, and the
comment ID and time in a private GitHub repository. The acceptance comment itself
is public on GitHub.

We receive purchase and license information from Polar. If an employer or client
assigns you a seat, we may also receive your contact and seat-assignment details
from that organization. This does not give us access to local captures or History.
For indirectly received details, we provide this notice with the first license
or activation communication, or earlier where legally required.

The app automatically checks Polar's license status to determine the commercial
rights shown in the app. A recognized rejection can restrict that status; network

assess your credit, employment or personal characteristics.

You can contact support@isoboard.app to request human review, explain your
circumstances and challenge an error. Payment cancellations are subject to the
verification, written notice and seat-specific safeguards in our

price-only adjustments do not by themselves justify revocations we initiate.
Polar may independently refund payments and revoke benefits before we can give
notice; once aware, we promptly notify you and review the change. We promptly correct errors or restore
rights after payment is restored, preserving the original version coverage and
support start date. Ending paid rights does not delete your data or prevent
otherwise compliant personal use.

## Retention

- Ordinary support correspondence: 12 months after resolution.
- Sensitive troubleshooting attachments: deleted within 30 days after resolution,
  unless needed for an active dispute.
- Minimal entitlement records: while needed to fulfil continuing license and
  recovery obligations, followed by any applicable legal or claims retention.
- Essential contribution/license agreement records: while needed to establish
  the rights relied on, followed by applicable legal or claims retention.
- Financial records: we retain the records necessary to document purchases,
  payouts, refunds and related accounting and tax obligations, normally for six
  years from the last entry in the relevant accounting books. Where a longer
  legal retention period applies, or specific records are necessary for an
  unresolved tax matter or legal claim, we retain those records for the applicable
  period or until that need ends. We delete or anonymize personal data when it is
  no longer needed, subject to any legally required restricted retention.
- Website analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics makes the previous six months of
  analytics history available to us, subject to the regional exclusion described
  above.
- Website security analytics: Cloudflare documents 31 days of history for its
  Security Events and Security Analytics datasets on our Free plan. We have not
  enabled separate log storage or exports. These history periods describe the
  data available to us, not a guarantee that all provider-internal copies are
  deleted at the same time.
- Other hosting/media records and activation logs: we do not maintain separate server-side activation logs or export Cloudflare request logs. License records remain in Polar for continuing licensing/recovery and applicable legal record-keeping purposes described above.

Our mailbox retention periods include deleted-mail folders. Polar, Cloudflare
and Apple may retain internal operational, backup or legally required copies under
their own terms; the periods available to us are not guarantees about every
provider-held copy. Contact legal@isoboard.app for retention information or to
exercise your rights; we coordinate with the provider where appropriate.

The support periods do not apply indiscriminately to accounting records or
minimal evidence needed for continuing licenses. An active dispute may justify
retaining necessary material longer; it does not justify keeping unrelated data.
Device-only History follows the storage limits and user controls described above.

## Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or
portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where
processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting prior lawful
processing. Contact legal@isoboard.app.

We normally respond within one month. Where a lawful extension is necessary due
to complexity or volume, we explain the reasons within that month; the extension
may be up to two further months. We request proportionate identity evidence only
where reasonably necessary. A refusal will explain the reasons and available
complaint and judicial remedies.

You may complain to the [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es/en)
or another competent supervisory authority. We cannot remotely delete local-only
History we do not hold, but can explain the local controls and address copies you
sent us. We may retain information required for a lawful continuing purpose.
