# isoboard support policy

Version: isoboard-terms-v2 · Effective date: 2026-11-13.
[Español](es/SUPPORT.md)

## Priority email support

An isoboard commercial purchase includes 12 months of priority email support
from license issuance. Contact support@isoboard.app. Priority means prioritized
handling; it does not promise a response deadline or guaranteed resolution.

Included assistance covers official releases, installation, configuration,
licensing and reproducible defects. Please include your app and operating-system
versions, a description of the problem, and steps to reproduce it. For purchase
or entitlement questions, include the order reference and purchase email.

The commercial-use entitlement covers isoboard code in modified builds. Support may
require reproducing a problem in an official release. Custom development,
custom-code debugging and unlimited troubleshooting are not included. External
AI-provider accounts and billing are handled by those providers; their charges
are separate from isoboard's price.

Please use a minimal example where possible. Remove confidential content and
credentials from material you send; never post license keys or payment details
in a public issue. Support may request further relevant information to diagnose
an issue, but no automatic access to your screenshots or documents is granted
by purchasing a license.

Priority support ends 12 calendar months after license issuance.
Reinstalling the app, activating another device, recovering the same license or
rotating its key does not restart the support period.

## After priority support expires

You retain the commercial-use entitlement for the purchased major version,
subject to the commercial terms. Billing, refund and license-recovery help
remain available through the support address. No separate priority-support
renewal is offered at launch.

## Community and licensing enquiries

Public bugs and feature requests can be submitted through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/carlesonielfa/isoboard-app/issues), with
best-effort responses. A feature request is not a commitment to implement it.
Private purchase or account information belongs in email, not public issues.

Use legal@isoboard.app for licensing, redistribution and integration
enquiries. Refunds follow the [purchase and refund terms](PURCHASE-REFUNDS.md).
Nothing in this support policy reduces mandatory legal remedies or makes them
conditional on an active priority-support period.
