# System requirements

- The first release targets macOS 14 or later. Use an installer built for your Mac's processor architecture.
- Screen capture requires the relevant macOS Screen Recording permission.
- Local inference requires downloading the selected model and enough free disk space and memory to load it. Requirements and performance depend on the model and your Mac.
- Remote AI providers require internet access and your own supported account or credentials. Their charges and service limits are separate.
- Commercial activation requires an internet connection to Polar. After activation, cached entitlement supports offline use; network failures do not remove it.

For compatibility questions before buying, contact support@isoboard.app.
